IANA timezone id used to present catch-up days and program times (default: Pacific/Noumea, New Caledonia / UTC+11).
When enabled, opening a catch-up capable channel plays what the provider broadcast at the same local wall-clock time (e.g. France 2's "20h" shown at 20:00 Caledonian time). Channels without catch-up support are hidden from Live TV while this is enabled.
