IANA timezone id used to present catch-up days and program times (default: Pacific/Noumea, New Caledonia / UTC+11).
When enabled, opening a catch-up capable channel plays what the provider broadcast at the same local wall-clock time (e.g. France 2's "20h" shown at 20:00 Caledonian time). Channels without catch-up support are hidden from Live TV while this is enabled.
Fills the restream buffer for this many seconds before live playback starts. The deliberate delay absorbs provider micro-drops (reconnects) at the cost of starting that far behind the real live edge. 0 disables. Default: 12.
Selected channels that are the same channel in different qualities (e.g. "FR| TF1 HD", "FR| TF1 FHD", "FR| TF1 4K", same country prefix) are shown as a single Live TV channel. Every quality found in the provider catalogue, selected or not, stays available as a source of that channel.

Channel selection

One line per channel, whatever its qualities. Ticking a channel enables it once in Live TV with every quality the provider offers (HD, FHD, 4K, …) as a source, and its catch-up archive when there is one, so it can be served at Caledonian time.
Prefix of the provider categories to list, e.g. FR for the "FR| …" categories.
